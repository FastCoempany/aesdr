The fork

AE path or SDR path?

Half the curriculum is shared. The other half is built around the specific job you're doing today. Here's what each path actually contains.

Lessons 1–5 · Foundation Both paths share the same fundamentals: sales register, prospect math, written communication, sequence design, calendar discipline. If you take both paths back-to-back, you don't re-take these.

SDR Path

You're in pipeline-gen.

SDR-specific lessons (18 sub-lessons)

06Cold outbound mechanics. Sequence opener math, reply-rate floors.
07The 30% rule. Why most cold lists are dead before you start.
08Manager 1:1 rubric. Five questions to bring; what your manager owes back.
09Ramp survival. Months 0–4: what good looks like; what to ignore.
10Pipeline-gen math. How much is enough; when to stop sending; comp implications.
11The AE handoff. What good qualification looks like; the AE/SDR alignment contract.
12SDR → AE jump. When you're ready; how the comp math actually changes.
You leave with: a survival playbook for first-12-months, a manager-1:1 framework, and the math for whether your pipeline-gen targets are real.

AE Path

You're carrying the bag.

AE-specific lessons (18 sub-lessons)

06Discovery deep-dive. The MEDDIC/MEDDPICC argument; what gets cut.
07The demo teardown. Why custom demos lose; what to do instead.
08The 30% rule (AE). How to read deal-stage math against your forecast.
09Pipeline-coverage math. 3x is a lie; what your real number is.
10Comp-plan teardown. Reading your plan; spotting the trap clauses; negotiating year two.
11The relationship graph. The five-year contacts that pay later; how to maintain them.
12Year-three resilience. When the comp plan changes mid-year; what to do.
You leave with: a pipeline-coverage formula that matches reality, a comp-plan reading habit, and the relationship discipline that pays off in years two and three.

Doing both jobs at once (player-coach SDR, AE who still does outbound)? Pick the role you spend more time in. You can switch primary path anytime; the non-primary content stays unlocked.
