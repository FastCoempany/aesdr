Mockups · Role-Path Discovery

Five approaches.
One conversion problem.

If a buyer skips the animation or scrolls straight to the pricing cards, they never learn the AE and SDR paths are different curricula, not just different examples. Each mockup below is a different way to close that gap. Click into any one to see it rendered.

A
Yours · Refined
Skip → "Who are you?" modal
When the user clicks "skip animation," a modal appears asking for role + buyer-type. Catches the skip case directly; risks reading as "I told you I was in a hurry."
Cost ~45 minFriction High
→
B
Yours · Refined
Click button on pricing cards
Tap-friendly button on each card opens a popover with the AE-vs-SDR-vs-Team breakdown. Information at the conversion moment. Converted from hover (broken on mobile) to click.
Cost ~30 minFriction Low
→

Mine
Sticky role-filter banner
Thin bar pins to the viewport top after the user scrolls past the hero. One click filters the entire page. Ambient navigation; never blocks content.
Cost ~1 hrFriction None

AE / SDR / Both tabs at the top of each card. Tap a tab → card content changes to the role-specific lessons, outcomes, and price. Decision happens at the moment of buying.
