The Manager Archetype Map

Most managers run on one operating system under pressure. Read which one yours defaults to, then use the matching weekly move to manage up on purpose — before their assumptions about you write your review for you.

How to use this
Find the archetype your manager runs on this quarter — most people are one type with a second showing up under stress. Read what that type reads as competence and what it reads as a threat. Then run the weekly move for that type, every week, in writing. You are not changing your manager. You are removing the gaps they would otherwise fill with their own guesses.
The five archetypes
01
Archetype 01
The Coach
Asks before telling. Develops you instead of just measuring you.
Reads as competence
  • You came with the question, not the complaint.
  • You can name your own gap.
  • You tried something before asking.
Reads as threat
  • You wait to be told.
  • You bring problems with no attempt attached.
Your weekly move

End your 1:1 with one decision you made this week, one you’re stuck on, and the option you’re leaning toward.

02
Archetype 02
The Metric Maniac
The dashboard is the conversation. Equates activity with output.
Reads as competence
  • Pipeline math that reconciles.
  • Activity tied to a specific stage gap.
  • Numbers that come with a story.
Reads as threat
  • Vibes-based updates.
  • “Working on it.”
  • A quiet day with nothing logged.
Your weekly move

Send a Friday five-line — last week’s number, this week’s, the gap, what’s closing it, what isn’t. Lead with the metric they watch most.

03
Archetype 03
The Template Tyrant
One script, one sequence, one way. No room for your judgment.
Reads as competence
  • You follow the process and bring the data on where you deviated and why it worked.
Reads as threat
  • Going off-script silently.
  • “I just did my own thing.”
Your weekly move

When you want to deviate, bring an A/B: “The template gets 4%, my variant got 9% on 40 sends. Can I run it on the next batch?”

04
Archetype 04
The Two-Faced Cheerleader
Public praise, private criticism. You never quite know where you stand.
Reads as competence
  • You create a paper trail — documented feedback, written recaps of every 1:1.
Reads as threat
  • Verbal-only agreements they can reframe later.
Your weekly move

After every 1:1, send a two-line written recap of what was agreed. Make the record exist.

05
Archetype 05
The Ghost
Absent. Unavailable. You’re coaching yourself.
Reads as competence
  • You operate without hand-holding.
  • You escalate only what genuinely needs them.
Reads as threat
  • Constant pings.
  • Needing them in every decision.
Your weekly move

Send one async update they can read in 30 seconds, ending with one specific question and a deadline. Make engaging with you cheap.

You are not trying to change your manager. You are removing the gaps they fill with assumptions about you.
