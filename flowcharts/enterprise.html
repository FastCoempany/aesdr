⚠ Where the rails stop

  1. No automated outbound to channel partners. The whole motion is one inbound form + you. (Compare to the affiliate machine, which has a full relay.)
  2. Herald isn't wired into the app — it can research and draft channel-partner approaches in chat, but nothing connects it to a queue or a sender yet.
  3. Steps C–D are off-system — the biggest opportunity if you ever want this to scale like the affiliate side.
