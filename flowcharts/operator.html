The whole point

Everything in grey is a machine working a backlog on a timer. The only two things that ever commit — spend a token-budget reach-out, send a real email to a real person — are Promote and Approve (Send). That's where you live.

Heads-up: every partner-side agent here is OFF by default — each has a master switch in the tower. Nothing sends until you turn it on.
