## What is locked vs free

- **Free (first two sentences):** naming the competitor honestly, and the specific reason AESDR is the better fit for their people. Be precise; "better-made" has to be backed by the detail.
- **Locked (everything after):** the economics, the run-both invitation, the sign-off.
- **No trash-talk.** We do not run down the competitor — we name that they already promote one, respect that, and let the product stand on its own. Confidence, not contempt.
- **The ask is the kit, not exclusivity.** Let them run both. A candidate who switches after comparing is worth ten who were argued into it.
