## What is locked vs free

- **Free (first two sentences):** the community name and why its members are the audience. Reference something real about the room if you can.
- **Locked (everything after):** the economics, the copy-review gate, the ask, the sign-off.
- **Say the gate out loud.** A community owner is renting you their members' trust. The review sentence is a feature here, not fine print — it is the reason they say yes.
- **The ask is a call.** Community owners decide relationally; fifteen minutes converts better than a kit drop for this one.
