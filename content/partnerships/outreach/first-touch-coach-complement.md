## What is locked vs free

- **Free (first two sentences):** the thing they made, and the specific foundation work AESDR takes off their plate. Name a real gap their clients hit.
- **Locked (everything after):** the product line, the economics, the ask, the sign-off.
- **Lead with what AESDR does, never with the price.** A coach chose their pricing on purpose; opening with "include the lower-priced thing" reads as the wrong kind of offer. The order is: what it handles → who it frees them to be → then, as a fact, the terms.
- **Their coaching is the higher-value relationship; AESDR is the groundwork beneath it.** Frame them as the expert whose hours get more valuable, not as a reseller.
