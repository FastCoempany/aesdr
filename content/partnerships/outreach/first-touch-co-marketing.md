## What is locked vs free

- **Free (first two sentences):** the piece or show that proves audience alignment, and which collaboration shape fits them. Pick one concrete format, do not list all three as a menu.
- **Locked (everything after):** the product line, the economics, the ask, the sign-off.
- **Lead with the collaboration, not the commission.** The affiliate terms appear once, as a fact, after the real offer. If the message reads as "here is a link," it is the wrong template.
- **The workshop system is already built** (`/[slug]/workshop`). A co-hosted workshop converts far better than a link drop, and it is the strongest version of this motion.
