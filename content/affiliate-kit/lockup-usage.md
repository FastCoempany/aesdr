# Lockup Usage Rules

The **AESDR × Affiliate** lockup is the co-brand mark used on affiliate-specific
registration pages, decks, and emails. The lockup files themselves are built
to your affiliate mark and arrive after the partnership agreement is signed —
this page is the rules they ship with.

## Where it goes

- **Registration page:** top-center, above the fold.
- **Workshop deck:** top-left of every slide template.
- **Affiliate-specific email:** footer only — never the header.

The lockup signals shared authorship of the workshop. It is not a logo
placement; it's an operator credit.

## Color

- AESDR mark in `--ink` (`#1A1A1A`).
- Affiliate mark in your brand color. AESDR has no opinion on yours.
- The `×` separator in `--ink` at matched x-height.

## Spacing

Minimum 24px clearspace around the lockup on all sides. Never inside a
colored panel that fights either brand. If the surface forces a tight crop,
shrink the lockup before you shrink the clearspace.

## Three things never to do

1. **No "presented by" / "in association with" / "powered by."** The lockup
   is the credit. Adding language defeats it.
2. **No third logo.** AESDR × Affiliate is two marks, not three. If a
   sponsor or platform mark is required by your channel, place it in a
   separate band, not inside the lockup.
3. **No proportion edits.** Don't squish, stretch, or recompose the supplied
   files. If a placement needs an aspect ratio we didn't ship, ask — we'll
   build it.

## What you'll receive after signing

Three lockup files:

- **Horizontal** — AESDR and your mark side-by-side on one line, with the
  `×` separator. Used at top-center of the registration page and the
  workshop deck slide template.
- **Stacked** — AESDR above your mark, two lines, separator centered.
  Used in tight-aspect placements like email footers, social profile
  squares, and small-format print.
- **On-cream** — same horizontal composition, optimized for printing or
  embedding into PDFs and slide decks with light backgrounds.

Plus a one-page composition guide that shows you exactly where the mark
sits on your registration page out of the gate, with the clearspace
already worked out.
