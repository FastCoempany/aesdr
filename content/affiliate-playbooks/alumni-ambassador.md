# Alumni ambassador

**Draft — co-written on request.**

You went through AESDR yourself and want to refer peers. The copy register is materially different from the other playbooks — testimony, not recommendation — and the disclosure framing has its own rules. We co-write this one with each alumni because the specific lens you took from the curriculum is what makes your testimony land, and a generic version would flatten exactly the thing your network would respond to.

## What we'd cover in a co-written playbook

- The testimony format: what changed for you, what didn't, what you'd tell yourself before you started
- The LinkedIn case-study format (typical: 600–1,000 word post, runs as one of your normal posts, not a sponsored one)
- The 1:1 DM format for peers in your network who've asked you the same question you came in with
- How to talk about the program with current AESDR cohort members without crossing into solicitation inside their cohort space
- FTC disclosure tuned for testimony-format content (the rules differ from recommendation-format)
- What to do when someone replies *"so you're getting paid to say this?"* — the honest answer, not the defensive one

## To get yours

Email `affiliates@aesdr.com` with:
- Your cohort or completion date
- The one thing from the program you'd anchor your testimony on
- Where your peer network mostly lives (LinkedIn, a specific Slack, a community, etc.)
- A draft of how you'd describe AESDR to a peer in 3–4 sentences, in your own words

We'll co-write inside 5 business days, send back a structured playbook, and review your first post before you publish.

In the meantime: don't run a generic-recommendation campaign as an alumnus. The leverage of the alumni path is that you actually went through it — using the same template a non-alumni affiliate would use throws that leverage away. Wait the week.
