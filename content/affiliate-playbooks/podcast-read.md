# Podcast read

**Draft — co-written on request.**

Mid-roll reads, full-episode integrations, and interview formats need a script and a measurement model the four ready playbooks don't cover. We co-write podcast playbooks per show because the right format depends on your audience's tolerance for ad-spots, the typical episode length, and whether you read or your producer reads.

## What we'd cover in a co-written playbook

- The 60–90 second mid-roll script (we draft, you adapt to your voice)
- The 3–5 minute "interview Antaeus" format if you want a deeper integration
- How to track without a vanity URL (you'll get a podcast-specific tracking slug — the `aesdr.io` shortener will host it once that's live)
- Cadence (typical: two reads two episodes apart, then dormant for a quarter)
- Show-note disclosure rules and the FTC requirement for the audible read itself
- What to do if a listener emails you with a question about the program

## To get yours

Email `affiliates@aesdr.com` with:
- Your podcast name and a link to a representative episode
- Approximate downloads per episode
- Read format you're considering (mid-roll, host-read interview, full episode)
- Your normal ad-spot cadence (or note that you don't currently run ads)

We'll co-write inside 5 business days, send back a script, and set up the tracking URL.

In the meantime: hold off. A poorly-tracked podcast read is the easiest way to undercount your conversions and feel underpaid — better to wait the week for the tracking setup than to run a read that doesn't attribute.
