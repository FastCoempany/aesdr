# Workshop host

A live 60-minute workshop, run with AESDR, promoted to your audience, with replay open for seventy-two hours after. The path with the highest conversion in the program — and the most coordination cost. Worth running once per quarter if it fits your audience; not worth forcing if it doesn't.

## Who this is for

- Newsletter list 5,000+, or
- Paid community 100+ members with consistent engagement, or
- Live-stream / regular audience of 200+ recurring viewers, or
- 1:1 client base of 20+ who'd show up because you asked.

If you're below those bars, the [Newsletter feature](/affiliates/dashboard/playbooks/newsletter-feature) or [Coach endorsement](/affiliates/dashboard/playbooks/coach-endorsement) playbooks earn you more for less coordination. Come back when the audience is there.


## Quick wins, first 14 days post-approval

1. Email `affiliates@aesdr.com` with your proposed date and a one-line audience descriptor. Format: `Date: 2026-XX-XX  ·  Audience: first-1-to-2-year SDRs in mid-market SaaS.` We'll confirm or counter within 24 business hours.
2. Read the [Pre-Workshop Checklist](/affiliates/kit-private/pre-workshop-checklist) end-to-end before you commit to the date. Most of the regret on a first pilot is about not knowing what you signed up for.
3. Block four hours on your calendar for the live: 30 min setup, 60 min workshop, 30 min Q&A overflow, 90 min decompression / debrief. Don't book anything against it.

## What the first asset is

The **save-the-date email**. Sent T-28 days to your full audience. The promo-copy-pack has the template (look for "01a — save-the-date — warm list"). Edit only the bracketed slots; leave the disclosure line untouched.

Don't customize the disclosure. We've cleared the wording with counsel and it's the same wording every affiliate uses. If you want a different angle on the body copy, that's fine — submit it through [Submit Copy](/affiliates/dashboard/submissions) and we'll review within 24 business hours.

## What to measure

| Stage | Baseline | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Registrations / list size | 5–8% for warm lists, 2–4% for colder | 3% is fine on a first pilot. 8% means your audience matches and you should run this twice a year. |
| Live attendance / registrations | 50–65% | Below 40% = the reminder cadence broke. Above 70% = you have an unusually committed audience. |
| Enrollments / live attendees | 6–12% | The number you actually care about. Below 4% = audience-fit debrief. |
| Refund rate, 30-day | 8–12% industry baseline | Above 15% = co-investigate. The refund window protects you AND the buyer. |

Numbers live in your [dashboard overview](/affiliates/dashboard) once registrations open.

## Cadence

**One workshop per quarter is sustainable.** Two is intense. Three or more becomes your part-time job, and that's a different conversation about co-promoted programming, not affiliate workshops.

Re-running the same workshop to the same audience inside 6 months: don't. Re-run to a different audience (a new community joined, a list grew, a co-promotion expanded the room): fine.

## FTC and disclosure

Three places the disclosure has to appear, all auto-handled or template-handled:

1. **The workshop registration page** — disclosure auto-renders at the bottom from your affiliate record. You don't write this.
2. **Your promo emails** — every template in the promo-copy-pack ships with the disclosure line baked in. Don't strip it.
3. **Verbal, in the workshop intro** — about 10 seconds: *"I earn a commission if anyone enrolls through my link. It doesn't change the price or the content. I'm recommending this because I think it'll help, not because of the commission."* Read it as written or paraphrase honestly. Don't skip it.
