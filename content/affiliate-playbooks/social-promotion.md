# Social-only promotion

**Draft — co-written on request.**

If your only channel is Twitter, LinkedIn, or short-video — no list, no community, no live audience yet — the path looks different enough from the four ready playbooks that it warrants its own. We haven't written it as a fixed playbook because the right cadence and angle vary so heavily by platform and following size that a generic version would be wrong for most affiliates running it.

## What we'd cover in a co-written playbook

- Honest baselines on per-post conversion (significantly lower than newsletter or workshop; the path runs on volume)
- Platform-specific cadence (Twitter saturation curve vs. LinkedIn vs. TikTok)
- The angle types that work without burning your audience's patience
- Whether to thread, single-post, or carousel — by platform
- FTC disclosure done right per platform (the rules differ; what's compliant on LinkedIn isn't enough on TikTok)
- When to stop running the path and switch to building a list

## To get yours

Email `affiliates@aesdr.com` with:
- Your primary platform and your handle
- Approximate following / monthly impressions
- A link to one or two posts you'd consider representative of your voice
- One sentence on what you're hoping the path looks like

We'll co-write the playbook with you inside 5 business days and ship it back as a private doc — and the patterns that show up across multiple affiliates running this path become the public version of this page.

In the meantime: the [promo-copy-pack](/affiliates/kit-private/promo-copy-pack) has social-format templates (sections 05a–05d) that work as a starting point. The disclosure rules in the [Newsletter feature](/affiliates/dashboard/playbooks/newsletter-feature) playbook carry over.
